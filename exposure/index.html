About this model & sources

This trial models direct printing onto paper or fabric. Choose a UV source above or below; the film and flat substrate side face the light in both setups. Vacuum-frame lids and covers are omitted in this simplified view. Film opacity, emulsion, drying and exposure are assumed suitable. The animation shows the process, not calibrated exposure times or measured print quality. With poor film contact, angled light can harden emulsion underneath image edges; fine detail may narrow or be lost. The optional “Show possible detail loss” effect illustrates this risk. It is off by default and does not predict the amount of loss in a real setup. Blue shading represents emulsion hardening; it is a teaching cue, not a predicted colour change.

Both sides are wetted before washout. This sequence develops the image from the flat substrate side, then turns the washed screen so both sides can be inspected. The dark artwork blocks UV; the protected emulsion stays washable until development.
